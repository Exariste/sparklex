About SparkleX

Built on one idea:
a truly clean home shouldn't be complicated.

SparkleX Home Care began with a simple frustration — everyday cleaning products that promised a lot and delivered little. Today, every bottle we make is formulated to cut grease and grime on contact while staying gentle on hands, fabric, and surfaces, so families across Bangladesh can spend less time cleaning and more time living.

FAQ

Common questions

Are SparkleX products safe for homes with children and pets?

Yes. All SparkleX formulas are dermatologically tested and formulated to be safe for family use when used as directed on the packaging.

Where are SparkleX products manufactured?

SparkleX products are manufactured in Kushtia, Bangladesh, under strict quality control as part of the EXARISTE GROUP.

How can I become a SparkleX distributor?

Fill out the application form in our Distributor Program section, or contact us directly via phone or WhatsApp. Our team will reach out within 2 business days.

Do you supply to hotels, hospitals, and other businesses?

Yes, we offer bulk supply and commercial pricing for hotels, hospitals, schools, restaurants, offices, and industrial clients.

Are your packaging materials recyclable?

We're actively moving toward fully recyclable and reduced-plastic packaging across the SparkleX range as part of our sustainability commitment.

Where can I buy SparkleX products?

SparkleX products are available through our authorized distributor and dealer network across Bangladesh. Contact us for your nearest stockist.
